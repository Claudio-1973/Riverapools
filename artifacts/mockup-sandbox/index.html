Direct Answers: Riverside County Pool Remodeling

What is the best pool finish for desert & Inland Empire climates?

Direct Answer: Pebble aggregate (such as StoneScapes) is the #1 recommended finish for Riverside County and Inland Empire climates. It withstands 100°F+ summer heat, intense UV rays, and hard tap water (15–26 grains/gal) without the etching, staining, or delamination common to traditional plaster. Quartz is the second-best choice for a smoother feel at a moderate price.

How much does pool replastering cost in Riverside County?

Direct Answer: Standard replastering costs between $4,500 and $7,500 for traditional white plaster, $6,000 to $9,500 for quartz aggregate (Diamond Brite), and $8,500 to $13,500 for StoneScapes pebble finishes for an average 15,000-gallon pool. Free itemized on-site quotes: (951) 345-9276.

How long does a pool remodel or resurfacing take?

Direct Answer: Standard pool resurfacing takes 5 to 7 business days from water drainage to refilling. A complete pool remodel with new travertine coping, waterline tile, and equipment upgrades takes 2 to 3 weeks.

How do I know if my pool plaster is delaminating?

Direct Answer: Four warning signs of delamination: 1) Hollow sound when tapped underwater with a pole; 2) Soft blisters or raised bumps on walls/floor; 3) Flaking plaster exposing rough gray gunite; and 4) Calcium nodules weeping through surface hairline cracks.

Project Timeline: Day-by-Day Breakdown

Day 1: Draining & Shell Sounding

Complete pool water drainage, hydrostatic plug relief, and comprehensive acoustic hammer testing to locate hollow delamination pockets.

Day 2: Chip-Out & Bond Coat Prep

Pneumatic chipping around tiles, fittings, and hollow areas down to gunite. High-strength polymer-modified bond coat application for mechanical keying.

Days 3–4: Hand Application & Acid Detail

Hand troweling of StoneScapes pebble or quartz finish, timed surface hydration, aggregate exposure wash, and light acid neutralization wash.

Days 5–7: Fill & 28-Day NPC Startup

Continuous freshwater fill to avoid waterline rings, initial chemical balancing, brushing protocol setup, and startup guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions: Riverside Pool Resurfacing & Remodeling

How much does pool replastering cost in Riverside County?

Pool replastering in Riverside County costs between $4,500 and $7,500 for standard white plaster, $6,000 to $9,500 for quartz aggregate (Diamond Brite), and $8,500 to $13,500 for StoneScapes pebble finishes for an average 15,000-gallon pool. The final estimate depends on pool perimeter, depth, and whether a complete pneumatic chip-out is required.

How long does a pool remodel or resurfacing take?

A standard replastering project takes 5 to 7 business days from water drainage to refill. A complete remodel involving natural travertine coping, waterline tile, Baja shelf additions, and equipment automation typically takes 2 to 3 weeks.

Which pool finish is best for Riverside's climate: Quartz or Pebble?

For the Inland Empire's high-heat desert climate, Pebble (StoneScapes) is the #1 choice: it offers 15 to 20+ years of lifespan and resists hard tap water scaling (15–26 grains/gal). Quartz (Diamond Brite) is an excellent mid-tier choice lasting 10 to 14 years with a smoother foot feel at 20% to 30% lower cost.

How do I know if my pool plaster is delaminating?

Four definitive signs of delamination: 1) A hollow 'popping' sound when tapped underwater with a service pole; 2) Visible raised blisters or lumps on walls and floor; 3) Flaking or peeling plaster exposing rough gray gunite; and 4) Calcium carbonate nodules weeping through fine cracks.
