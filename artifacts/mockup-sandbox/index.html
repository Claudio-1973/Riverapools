Our Pool Remodeling Process

  1. Free site assessment: We inspect the pool, take measurements, discuss problems, and clarify the desired result.
  2. Materials and design: The homeowner compares plaster, quartz, pebble, coping, tile, lighting, and equipment options.
  3. Preparation and installation: Our crews drain, prepare, repair, and apply the selected materials using the agreed scope.
  4. Fill and startup: We refill the pool, balance the water, explain initial care, and review the finished work.

Pool Finishes, Repairs, and Backyard Upgrades

A resurfacing project can restore a comfortable, watertight surface and update the water color. Coping and waterline tile can modernize the pool edge, while Baja shelves, LED lighting, pumps, heaters, and automation can improve daily use. When plaster is hollow, lifting, cracked, stained, or unusually rough, we evaluate whether localized repair or complete resurfacing is the more durable solution.
