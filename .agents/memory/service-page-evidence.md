---
name: Service-page evidence
description: How service landing pages should describe portfolio photos and documented projects.
---

Use portfolio photos as representative examples unless the project record explicitly documents the service, scope, and location being claimed. A documented case study may be reused for comparison, but its limits should be stated when it does not prove the current service.

**Why:** Search-focused service pages need concrete project evidence, but attributing an unverified leak, finish, repair, or city outcome to a photo creates a trust and accuracy problem.

**How to apply:** Keep alt text and body copy precise; distinguish documented project facts from general service guidance, and say when a case study is illustrative rather than service-specific.
